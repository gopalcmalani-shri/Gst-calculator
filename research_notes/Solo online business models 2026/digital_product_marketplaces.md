# Digital Product Marketplaces ("Upload Once, Sell Many Times") for a Solo AI-Assisted Creator, 2025–2026

Research date: 2026-11-18. Most findings come from search-result summaries and secondary blogs. I could not fetch teach.udemy.com: the session's network blocked the host. Treat every number tagged "(secondary)" as unverified against the platform's own page.

## Fee and royalty structures by platform

### Takeaway
Take rates run from about 0–10% on direct storefronts (Gumroad, Payhip, Beehiiv, Substack-in-India) to 63–85% on aggregator marketplaces (Udemy subscription, Adobe Stock, Envato Elements). On the aggregators, discovery is the thing you are paying for. Several platforms changed their terms in 2025–2026 (Udemy, Skillshare, KDP, Redbubble, Envato, Merch), and each change cut creators' share or added complexity.

### Cited Findings
**Online courses**
- Udemy subscription revenue share was cut on a schedule announced in 2023: 20% (Jan 2024), 17.5% (Jan 2025), 15% (Jan 2026). The marketplace (à-la-carte) instructor share of 37% for organic Udemy-driven sales is unchanged. Udemy's FY2025 report confirms 17.5% from 1 Jan 2025 and a further cut to 15% — [Udemy Teach blog (via search)](https://teach.udemy.com/enabling-investment-subscription-terms-update/); [iTiger FY2025 summary](https://www.itiger.com/news/2612233046)
- A Udemy community post refers to a separate "2.5% revenue share adjustment" in Jan 2026. I could not confirm it in an official document — [Udemy community](https://community.udemy.com/en/discussion/163733/the-slow-fade-of-udemy-a-platform-turning-its-back-on-the-people-who-built-it)
- A community thread says Coursera and Udemy combined, with completion around May 2026. I did not verify this in a primary source — [Udemy community Q&A thread](https://community.udemy.com/en/discussion/163453/share-your-questions-about-the-coursera-udemy-combination-here)
- Skillshare: since 1 Oct 2022 a monthly "Teacher Fund" set by Skillshare replaces the royalty pool. Pay is based on minutes watched plus engagement — [Skillshare Help (2023 update)](https://help.skillshare.com/hc/en-us/articles/12387192761357)
- Skillshare published a "Teacher Earnings Update (Effective January 1, 2026)" and new royalty-eligibility requirements from 16 July 2026. I could not retrieve the details — [Skillshare Help Jan 2026](https://help.skillshare.com/hc/en-us/articles/41694886825229-Skillshare-Teacher-Earnings-Update-Effective-January-1-2026); [Skillshare Help update](https://help.skillshare.com/hc/en-us/articles/46487542333837)
- Third-party per-minute estimates for Skillshare (~$0.05–0.10/min) are unsourced and conflict with each other — [course-coach (secondary)](https://course-coach.pages.dev/blog/udemy-vs-skillshare-which-pays-more/)

**Ebooks / low-content books**
- KDP ebooks: the 70% option requires a list price in the band and carries a delivery fee ($0.15/MB US). The 35% option has no delivery fee — [Vappingo (secondary)](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/amazon-kdp-royalties-35-vs-70/); [Kindlepreneur calculator](https://kindlepreneur.com/kdp-royalty-calculator/)
- One 2026 source says the Amazon.com 70% band was widened to $2.99–$12.99 as of 7 July 2026, from $2.99–$9.99. Only one secondary source reports this, so verify it — [Inkfluence AI (secondary)](https://www.inkfluenceai.com/blog/kdp-royalties-2026)
- KDP paperbacks: 60% at a list price of $9.99+ and 50% at $9.98 or below (a June 2025 change), minus printing cost (~$1.00 + $0.012/page for US B&W) — [BestWriting (secondary)](https://bestwriting.com/kdp-royalties); [Reedsy](https://blog.Reedsy.com/guide/kdp/amazon-self-publishing-royalties/)
- The KDP Select / Kindle Unlimited fund was $63.3M (May), $67.0M (June) and $69.4M (August) in 2026 — [KDP Earn page](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/earn)
- Google Play Books: 70% for partners on the updated ToS in most countries. A 52% default applies in "certain countries", and India was listed among the countries that keep the older split, so expect 52% on Indian sales — [Google Play Books Revenue Split FAQ](https://support.google.com/books/partner/answer/9331459?hl=en)

**Digital templates / storefronts**
- Etsy: $0.20 listing fee, 6.5% transaction fee on item price plus shipping, and payment processing of about 3% + $0.25 in the US. Offsite Ads take 15% of attributed sales for shops under $10k/yr (opt-out allowed) and 12% for shops at $10k+ (mandatory) — [Craftybase (secondary)](https://app.craftybase.com/blog/the-complete-guide-to-etsy-fees); [Printful blog](https://printful.com/blog/how-much-does-etsy-take-per-sale)
- Etsy India payment-processing rate: sources conflict (5% + ₹25, 0.05%, 0.29%). Gelato mentions a 0.29% India fee starting 1 Apr 2026 — [Gelato](https://www.gelato.com/vi/blog/etsy-fees); [Printify](https://printify.com/blog/how-much-does-etsy-take-per-sale/)
- Gumroad: 10% on direct sales and 30% on Discover-marketplace sales. It has been Merchant of Record since 1 Jan 2025 and handles global VAT/GST. Sources disagree on whether a $0.50 per-sale fee also applies — [Dodo Payments (secondary)](https://dodopayments.com/blogs/gumroad-fees-explained); [Checkoutpage (secondary)](https://checkoutpage.com/blog/gumroad-fees)
- Lemon Squeezy, a Gumroad alternative, is reported at 5% + $0.50 with MoR tax handling — [Fungies (secondary)](https://fungies.io/?p=37722)

**Stock assets**
- Adobe Stock: 33% royalty for photos, vectors, illustrations and generative AI content, and 35% for video, applied to net price. Large-subscription floors are $0.33 (0–999 lifetime licenses), $0.36 (1,000–9,999) and $0.38 (10,000+). The help page's worked example gives $0.99 per photo on a $29.99/10-image plan. Forum reports put typical earnings at $0.33–$1 per download — [Adobe Stock royalty details](https://helpx.adobe.com/africa/stock/contributor/help/royalty-details.html)
- Adobe pays an annual Firefly training bonus. The 2025 bonus covers content considered for training between 3 Jun 2024 and 2 Jun 2025. Contributors are also paid when their assets are used in "Customize" generative edits — [Adobe community announcement](https://community.adobe.com/announcements-37/announcing-the-2025-firefly-bonus-for-contributors-317076); [Adobe community Customize](https://community.adobe.com/t5/stock-contributors-discussions/adobe-announces-the-new-customize-experience-on-adobe-stock/td-p/15204112)
- Shutterstock pays a separate Contributor Fund from AI/data licensing deals, distributed every 3–6 months — [Shutterstock data licensing help](https://intercom.help/shutterstock-contributor/en/articles/10594694-shutterstock-data-licensing-and-the-contributor-fund); [TurboSquid blog](https://blog.turbosquid.com/?p=13272)
- A blog says average Shutterstock earnings are about $0.78/download in 2025, down from more than $1 a few years earlier (secondary, unverified) — [search summary of microstock blogs](https://xpiksapp-website.pages.dev/blog/high-barrier-niches-on-microstocks/)
- Envato Market: the author fee comes off the item price, with lower fees for established exclusive authors (as low as 12.5%, secondary). Minimum payout is $50 — [Envato Author Help](https://help.author.envato.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000424683); [PureThemes](https://purethemes.net/envato-author-exclusivity-merchant-of-record/)
- Envato Elements: authors get 50% of net subscription revenue, split by downloads, and 25% for Enterprise plans. New subscription tiers launched 25 Feb 2026 with the subscriber-share model kept. One blog's report of a "July 2026 50/50 Market split" is unverified — [Envato Elements tiers & share](https://help.author.envato.com/hc/en-us/articles/54407097667993-Envato-Elements-subscription-pricing-tiers-and-author-revenue-share); [ChargePanda (secondary)](https://www.chargepanda.com/blog/post/envato-july-2026-changes-wordpress-authors-guide)

**Print-on-demand**
- Redbubble introduced Standard/Premium/Pro tiers in September 2025 with a platform fee on Standard. No thresholds for moving between tiers are published (secondary) — [MyDesigns blog](https://mydesigns.io/blog/?p=3952)
- Merch by Amazon pays royalties as list price minus Amazon's costs and taxes. Accounts start at Tier 10 and upload tiers unlock with sales. "Royalty incentive groups" were reported from 1 Jun 2026 (one vendor blog, unverified) — [SellerSprite](https://www.sellersprite.com/en/blog/amazon-merch-royalties-and-pricing); [BeBold Digital](https://www.bebolddigital.com/blog/amazon-merch-tiers)

**Paid newsletters**
- Substack takes 10% of paid subscriptions, but currently waives the fee in India and Brazil. Indian creators need a Stripe account, and Stripe India is invite-only for new accounts — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/chat/h/31a40575-f12a-4aa5-93bc-60e6ff2c0f23/); [Beehiiv blog](https://www.beehiiv.com/blog/how-much-does-substack-cost)
- Beehiiv takes 0% but requires a paid plan to sell subscriptions (Scale ~$43/mo annual, $49 monthly). The break-even against Substack's 10% is about $490–$1,200/mo in paid revenue, depending on the plan. One source's "5% Beehiiv fee" is an outlier — [DevOpsCube](https://devopscube.com/beehiiv-vs-substack/); [EmailListValidation](https://emaillistvalidation.com/blog/substack-beehiiv-ghost-fees-paid-subscriptions-compared/)

**AI prompt marketplaces**
- PromptBase charges 0% when the buyer arrives via the seller's own link and 20% when the marketplace brings the buyer. The minimum payout is reported at $20–$30 — [Cartmango](https://cartmango.com/sell-ai-prompts/); [PromptBase blog](https://promptbase.com/blog/ai-creator-marketplace)
- The median prompt price is reported at $5.99 (secondary) — [Cartmango](https://cartmango.com/sell-ai-prompts/)

### Inferences
- On aggregators (Udemy subscription at 15%, Adobe Stock at 33%), per-unit income is cents to a few dollars. Volume is what matters there.
- On direct storefronts (Gumroad, Payhip, Beehiiv), the creator keeps 90–100%, but must bring the traffic themselves.
- For an India-based seller, Substack's waived fee and Gumroad/Lemon Squeezy's MoR tax handling cut the compliance burden.
- Google Play Books' 52% India rate is noticeably below the 70% elsewhere.

### Gaps
- Payhip's fee (widely known as 0%/2%/5% by plan) and Freepik contributor rates did not come up in the searches. They are unverified here.
- I found no official Etsy India processing rate. No current official page confirmed Udemy's marketplace 37%/97% split for instructor-referral coupons.
- No Merch by Amazon royalty percentages came from an official source.
- Music distribution (DistroKid/Spotify-type) royalties were not researched.

## AI-content policies by platform

### Takeaway
The platforms split into three groups:
- **Accept AI content with disclosure:** KDP, Adobe Stock, Etsy (as "Designed by"), Udemy (AI-assisted only, not fully AI-generated).
- **Ban AI contributor uploads:** Shutterstock, Pond5, AudioJungle/Envato (AI as the main component).
- **Restrict categories:** Etsy bans AI-generated patterns, recipes and prompt bundles.

Several platforms that accept AI content also cap volume (KDP's 3 titles/day) or demote it (Adobe hides AI images by default).

### Cited Findings
- KDP: disclosure is required for AI-generated text, images and translations. AI-assisted work (you wrote it, AI edited it) does not require disclosure. The daily cap is 3 new titles per account; Amazon confirmed the figure to The Guardian (Sept 2023) and said it may be adjusted — [Authors Guild](https://authorsguild.org/news/amazon-adds-to-kdp-generative-ai-policy-caps-daily-self-publishing-uploads/); [TechSpot](https://www.techspot.com/news/100246-amazon-puts-cap-self-publishing-combat-flood-ai.html); [ScribeCount](https://scribecount.com/author-resource/protecting-books/kdp-ai-content-policy-the-compliance-guide)
- Adobe Stock: has accepted labeled generative-AI content since Dec 2022. Since January 2025, customer searches default to "Exclude Generative AI". Adobe has restricted accounts in Indonesia after a crackdown on AI asset farming (secondary) — [CineD](https://www.cined.com/ai-flood-on-adobe-stock-nearly-half-of-all-images-now-ai-generated/); [Adobe community](https://community.adobe.com/t5/stock-contributors-discussions/attention-starting-today-adobe-stock-hides-ai-images-for-customers-by-default/m-p/15084517/highlight/true)
- Shutterstock: does not accept AI-generated content from contributors, citing the IP-ownership requirements in Contributor Terms §13d/13f. It licenses AI output only from its own generator. A Feb 2026 blog claiming Shutterstock accepts AI with disclosure contradicts the official help pages — [Shutterstock contributor FAQ](https://submit.shutterstock.com/help/en/articles/10594676-ai-generated-content-contributor-faq); [Shutterstock policy update](https://submit.shutterstock.com/help/en/articles/10594622-content-policy-updates-ai-generated-content)
- Pond5 (owned by Shutterstock): its January 2025 Creative Brief says it does not accept AI-generated content, whatever the tool, disclosure or degree of editing (secondary) — [Dynamoi](https://dynamoi.com/learn/ai-music-distribution/does-pond5-accept-ai-music); [Jack Righteous](https://jackrighteous.com/en-gb/blogs/jack-righteous-updates/pond5-ai-music-guide)
- AudioJungle/Envato: bans submissions where AI-generated content is the main component, such as Suno/Udio music. Breaking the rule risks termination and forfeiture of earnings (secondary) — [Dynamoi](https://dynamoi.com/learn/ai-music-distribution/does-audiojungle-accept-ai-music.html.md)
- Etsy:
  - Creativity Standards (July 2024) allow AI-assisted items labeled "Designed by a seller". AI use must be disclosed, and the seller must prompt and curate the output.
  - A rewrite on 10 June 2025 removed the "templated design or pattern" allowance.
  - AI-generated knitting, crochet and sewing patterns, recipes and assembly instructions are banned.
  - Prompt bundles are reported as banned (secondary).
  - Sellers report false AI flags. A claimed "38% false-flag rate / 17,000 removals" figure is unverified.
  - Sources: [Etsy House Rules](https://www.etsy.com/help/article/4511); [Value Added Resource](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/etsy-bans-ai-patterns/); [Listadum](https://www.listadum.com/blog/etsy-creativity-standards); [EcomCrew](https://www.ecomcrew.com/etsy-calls-itself-human-centered-its-sellers-have-a-more-complicated-story-to-tell/)
- Udemy:
  - Courses that are entirely AI-generated are not accepted, and AI cannot replace the instructor's expertise.
  - AI use must be disclosed in the course description, including AI translation and voice cloning.
  - Low-quality AI/TTS content is restricted.
  - Trust & Safety watches for voice and content impersonation.
  - A separate opt-in GenAI program licenses instructors' content for AI.
  - Sources: [Udemy Course Quality Checklist: Use of AI](https://support.udemy.com/hc/en-us/articles/30999984483607-Course-Quality-Checklist-Use-of-AI); [404 Media](https://404media.co/massive-e-learning-platform-udemy-gave-teachers-a-gen-ai-opt-out-window-its-already-over)
- Redbubble / Merch by Amazon: I found no authoritative AI-design policy text. Merch upload tiers depend on sales and account quality, and copyright strikes can terminate Redbubble accounts — [BeBold Digital](https://www.bebolddigital.com/blog/amazon-merch-tiers); [SimplyTrust](https://simplytrust.com/digital-assets/redbubble/)
- PromptBase publishes prompt submission guidelines (quality/originality) — [PromptBase guidelines](https://promptbase.com/prompt-guidelines)

### Inferences
- An AI-heavy workflow fits best on KDP (disclosed, ≤3/day), Adobe Stock (labeled, but hidden by default in search), Etsy printables/SVGs ("Designed by", disclosed), and Gumroad/Payhip (no AI restriction found).
- Pure AI-music or AI-footage stock is effectively closed at Pond5, AudioJungle and Shutterstock.

### Gaps
- No official Redbubble or Merch by Amazon AI policy was found.
- No Gumroad, Payhip, Substack or Beehiiv AI-content rules were found. They appear to have no specific AI ban, but I did not verify this.
- No Freepik contributor AI policy was found.

## Typical earnings distribution (median vs top sellers)

### Takeaway
Every category shows the same power law: the median or typical creator earns little (often under $100/month), and a small minority earns full-time income. Platform-wide pools (KDP KU about $65–70M/month, Etsy GMS $10.46B) are large, but they are spread across millions of sellers.

### Cited Findings
- Written Word Media 2025 author survey (n=1,346, self-reported): 44% earned ≤$100/month, 20% earned $500–$5,000, 13% earned more than $5,000 and 8% more than $10,000 — [Written Word Media 2025 survey](https://www.writtenwordmedia.com/2025-author-survey/)
- ALLi 2025 author income survey: median self-published author income was $13,500, up 6% YoY. The sample is limited to "qualifying" authors who spend more than half their working time on writing and publishing, so it is skewed upward — [ALLi / SelfPublishingAdvice](https://selfpublishingadvice.org/2025-author-income-survey/)
- Authors Guild (2023 survey of 2022 income): $10,000 median for full-time authors and $2,000 median book income across all authors — [Sudowrite summary](https://sudowrite.com/blog/how-much-do-authors-really-make-2025-data-on-royalties-advances-and-income/)
- Etsy FY2025: Etsy marketplace GMS was about $10.46B with 5.6M active marketplace sellers (down 1.5% YoY). That implies roughly $1,870 average GMS per seller per year, with the median far lower. I found no Etsy seller-income survey — [Etsy 10-K FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1370637/000137063726000019/etsy-20251231.htm); [Etsy Q4 2025 release](https://investors.etsy.com/_assets/_fea57334fe81a735b34cf3bf4bdcb553/etsy/db/938/10062/earnings_release/Exhibit+99.1+12.31.2025.pdf)
- KDP Select fund: $63.3M (May 2026), $67.0M (Jun) and $69.4M (Aug) — [KDP](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/earn)
- Adobe Stock per-download earnings are about $0.33–$1, and contributors report sudden drops in downloads in 2025–2026. Adobe said in Sep 2024 that contributor earnings were at an "all-time high" — [Adobe community](https://community.adobe.com/questions-38/drastic-decrease-in-monthly-earnings-317425); [Adobe blog Sep 2024](https://blog.adobe.com/en/publish/2024/09/10/adobe-stock-continued-commitments-to-creators)
- PromptBase: one artist said it initially added 30–40% to his monthly income, but that share fell as competition grew (anecdotal) — [Analytics India Magazine](https://analyticsindiamag.com/ai-trends/ai-artists-are-making-money-from-selling-prompts-rather-than-art)

### Inferences
- Assume a realistic year-1 outcome of $0–$100/month per marketplace for a new solo creator. Top-decile results ($5k+/month) usually come from catalogs built over years plus off-platform audiences.
- The ~$1.9k average GMS per Etsy seller is my own calculation from the 10-K numbers, not a published figure.

### Gaps
- No official or credible 2025 data was found on median Gumroad creator earnings, Udemy instructor earnings distribution, the Adobe Stock contributor distribution, Merch/Redbubble seller incomes, or Substack/Beehiiv paid-newsletter medians.
- No time-to-first-sale data with a credible source was found for any platform. Support burden and time to first sale were not covered by any cited source.

## Saturation since the AI boom, and what still sells

### Takeaway
AI content has flooded the low-effort categories: Adobe Stock was about 48% AI-generated by April 2025, KDP needed a daily title cap, and Etsy banned AI patterns. Platforms are responding by demoting or labeling AI work, banning it outright, or cutting payouts. The categories still holding up are high-barrier niches, products built on human expertise, and creators with their own audience.

### Cited Findings
- On 8 April 2025, 47.85% of Adobe Stock's image portfolio was generative AI — [CineD](https://www.cined.com/ai-flood-on-adobe-stock-nearly-half-of-all-images-now-ai-generated/)
- Adobe Stock contributors report download drops that came all at once rather than gradually, in 2025 and again in 2026 — [Adobe community 2026 thread](https://community.adobe.com/questions-38/has-anyone-else-experienced-a-sudden-drop-in-adobe-stock-downloads-in-2026-1630684); [Adobe community](https://community.adobe.com/questions-38/drop-in-sales-from-september-1612985)
- Adobe forum thread "similar content – the end of AI contributors" reports rejections of near-duplicate AI content — [Adobe community](https://community.adobe.com/questions-38/similar-content-the-end-of-ai-contributors-1552169)
- Commentary on microstock advises moving into "high-barrier niches" as stock shifts from content to data licensing (secondary) — [Xpiks](https://xpiksapp-website.pages.dev/blog/high-barrier-niches-on-microstocks/)
- Shutterstock's data/distribution revenue grew 16% to $203.3M while its core content business weakened (secondary). The Getty–Shutterstock merger reportedly fell through (July 2026 blog) — [Brutally Honest Microstock](https://brutallyhonestmicrostock.com/2026/07/04/getty-and-shutterstock-merger-falls-through-what-it-means-for-stock-contributors/)
- KDP's 3-titles/day cap was introduced specifically to combat the flood of AI-generated books — [TechSpot](https://www.techspot.com/news/100246-amazon-puts-cap-self-publishing-combat-flood-ai.html)
- Etsy's 2025 bans on AI patterns and recipes, and its tighter listing-photo rules, target AI spam — [Value Added Resource](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/etsy-bans-ai-patterns/)
- Udemy instructors describe falling income, citing subscription cuts and competition — [Udemy community](https://community.udemy.com/en/discussion/163733/the-slow-fade-of-udemy-a-platform-turning-its-back-on-the-people-who-built-it)
- Redbubble's Sept 2025 tier and fee system is reported to cut small sellers' earnings sharply (secondary) — [MyDesigns](https://mydesigns.io/blog/?p=3952)

### Inferences
- What still sells (inferred from policy direction and complaints): products that carry verifiable human expertise (courses, niche ebooks with an author platform), human-shot or high-barrier stock (editorial, real locations, people, video), and functional templates that solve a specific workflow problem.
- Direct-to-audience sales (newsletter + Gumroad/Payhip) avoid marketplace demotion of AI content.

### Gaps
- No quantitative data was found on the number of AI templates on Etsy or Notion/Canva marketplaces, or on KDP title-count growth after the AI boom.

## India-based seller support (non-tax)

### Takeaway
Most platforms covered accept India-based creators, but there are friction points: Stripe India being invite-only (which affects Substack and Beehiiv payouts), Google Play Books' lower 52% India split, and an unclear Etsy India processing fee. I verified only some of these.

### Cited Findings
- Substack: India fee waived, but a connected Stripe account is needed and Stripe India is invite-only for new accounts. Some Indian creators must request access — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/chat/h/31a40575-f12a-4aa5-93bc-60e6ff2c0f23/)
- Google Play Books: India remains on the 52% split — [Google FAQ](https://support.google.com/books/partner/answer/9331459?hl=en)
- Etsy: India-specific processing fees exist, but the reported figures conflict — [Gelato](https://www.gelato.com/vi/blog/etsy-fees)
- Gumroad's MoR model (from Jan 2025) handles buyer-side VAT/GST globally — [Dodo Payments](https://dodopayments.com/blogs/gumroad-fees-explained)

### Gaps
- No source in this research confirmed India eligibility for Udemy, KDP, Adobe Stock, Shutterstock, Envato, Redbubble, Merch by Amazon (historically invite/region-limited), Payhip, Beehiiv payouts or PromptBase. These need checking on each platform's payout or eligibility page.
